# Code of Conduct

Be professional. Critique code, rules, and ideas — never people. No
harassment, hate speech, doxxing, or personal attacks in any project space.

Disagreements about rules and design are resolved the AgentLintel way — with
evidence, fixtures, and ADRs, not volume.

Report conduct problems privately via the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove content, reject contributions, and warn or ban
accounts to keep the project safe. Severe or repeated violations mean
removal.
